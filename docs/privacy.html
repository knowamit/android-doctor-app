Privacy Policy: Android Doctor

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

Android Doctor runs its checks on your phone. The results of those checks stay on your phone. The app also includes third-party services from Google that collect some data: Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). This page lists what they collect.

Who we are

Android Doctor is developed by knowamit. Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/knowamit/android-doctor-app/issues.

What stays on your device

The app processes the following on your phone and does not upload it to us or to anyone else:

  • Diagnostics output: battery, storage, memory and CPU readings and the scores built from them
  • The list of installed apps, used by the storage and debloat screens
  • Usage statistics (when last each app was opened), only if you grant Usage access in Android Settings
  • Fix history and the previous values needed to undo a change
  • Benchmark results and score snapshots, stored in a local database
  • App preferences

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What third-party services collect

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app shows ads. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and your consent choices (through Google's User Messaging Platform). See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Firebase Analytics. Collects an app instance ID, automatically collected events (for example first open, session start, screen views and app updates), device model, OS version, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address (country and region), and the advertising ID when available. The app also logs these events: shizuku_granted, quick_scan_completed (with the score), diagnosis_started, diagnosis_completed (with the overall score) and fix_applied (with the level and the number of packages disabled).

Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, it collects the crash stack trace, the state of the device at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID.

Firebase data handling is described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the weekly health notice.
  • Stop background processes (KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES): to ask Android to release memory used by background apps.
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): optional, granted by you in Settings, to find apps you have not opened in a while.
  • Battery statistics (BATTERY_STATS): only effective when Shizuku is set up.
  • Package visibility: the app can see apps that have a launcher icon. It does not request the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
  • Permissions added by the ads and Firebase libraries: internet, network state and advertising ID.

Shizuku is optional. If you install and authorize it, the app runs shell commands with elevated permissions on your device. These commands run locally.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • Where required by law, change your ad consent at any time from Privacy options in the app's More menu.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local data.

Children's Privacy

Android Doctor is not directed to children under 13.
